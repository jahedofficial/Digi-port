# Knowledge Module: Google Ads Core Architecture & Best Practices
**Approved Source Category:** Google Official Documentation (`support.google.com/google-ads/`)

## 1. Overview
Google Ads captures high-intent demand through Search, Shopping, Performance Max, YouTube/Video, and Demand Gen campaigns.

## 2. Core Concepts & Campaign Types
- **Search Campaigns:** Keyword-driven intent capture using Broad Match + Smart Bidding or Exact/Phrase Match.
- **Performance Max (PMax):** Goal-based campaign type using multi-channel asset groups (text, images, video) across Search, Display, YouTube, Discover, Maps, and Gmail.
- **Shopping & Merchant Center:** Feed-driven inventory promotion for e-commerce products with automated product title optimization.

## 3. Smart Bidding Strategies
- **tROAS (Target ROAS):** Predicts future conversion value and maximizes revenue while targeting a specific return. Requires at least 15–30 conversions in the last 30 days.
- **tCPA (Target Cost Per Action):** Optimizes bids to get as many conversions as possible at or below the target CPA.
- **Maximize Conversions:** Best for spending full budget to gather baseline conversion volume.

## 4. Enhanced Conversions & Consent Mode v2
- Hashes first-party customer data (SHA-256) on conversion events and matches against signed-in Google accounts.
- Mandatory for accurate conversion modeling and Google Ads compliance under European DMA rules.
